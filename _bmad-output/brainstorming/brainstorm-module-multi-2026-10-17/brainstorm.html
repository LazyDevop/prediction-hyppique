Six combinations played

New dials for the selection

  • Bases by press consensus — horses with ≥ 5 of 6 tips become automatic base candidates in M2.
  • Bases = M1 ∩ B2; selection = M1 ∪ B2 (cut to size) — documentary method fused with the model.
  • M1-trot — M1 adapted to trotting, where D (disqualified) means something different (gait) than on the flat.
  • Allocation window — average prize money over 3 races, not just the last.
  • Stall / rail draw — stall-number bias per racecourse and distance (flat).
  • Jockey / driver — switching to a top jockey is a signal.
  • Rest — days since the last run (comeback, quick turnaround).

Controls for the punter

  • Progressive guarantee — mixed 5+6 systems shown as a guarantee slider (Multi en 5 guaranteed ↔ Multi en 6 guaranteed).
  • “Cautious ↔ bold” slider that drives k and the number of bases instead of exposing technical parameters.
  • Budget presets — Mini 15 € / Standard 30 € / Large 60 €, each with p_hit and the count of physical tickets.
  • Suggested swap — the user picks; the app proposes 1–2 swaps that gain the most p_hit.
  • Per-horse sensitivity — Δp_hit if removed: shows the horses that “carry” the system.
  • Systems sized to the physical slip — prefer multiples of 4 bets to fill the paper forms.

Modes & horizons

  • Strategy router — learn which method works in which race type (flat / jumps / trot, handicap, field size) and choose automatically.
  • Paper trading — the user logs the systems they would have played; the app tracks results with no money.
  • Weekly horizon — spread a budget over a week of support races rather than race by race.

Photo-finish: five insights

The camera at the post caught five noses on the line. The judges could not separate them.

Prix du Multi · 17.10.2026Finish camera · 1/10000 s
1

The free lever hidden in §5.5

The spec says ticket structure adds no EV. True on average — but choosing among equivalent optimal designs, and how they are relabelled, is a free value lever. Build it into the cache from V1.5.

POST
2

App users share one pool

An unseen tension: every app user bets into the same Multi pool, so one system recommended to all dilutes their winnings. Diversify per user — still deterministic if the seed is (race, user).

POST
3

The protocol is the pitch

The immutable log is not just a scientific tool: it is the trust product — timestamped proof, museum of failures, proof screen. The validation protocol IS the app’s argument.

POST
4

PMUC Cameroon, not PMU France

The underlying risk: the user bets through the local operator. rules.py must be indexed by operator (price, currency, rules), or the whole financial side is wrong.

POST
5

Human selections, no money

Paper trading + league + quiz yield human selections without stakes → a strategy U and faster validation data, in line with the principle “the tool never bets”.
