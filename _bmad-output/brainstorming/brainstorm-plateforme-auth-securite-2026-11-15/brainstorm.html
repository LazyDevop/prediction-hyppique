The weighing room: PIN and sessions

Triggered by a user decision: the phone may be shared, but the app is not.

The PIN

4-6 digits, optional biometrics, auto-lock after X minutes idle and on every return from background. It unlocks a token in Keystore / Keychain and never reaches the server.

5 wrong PINs: local session wiped, OTP login required.

The token chain

access 15 min→refresh #1→refresh #2→#1 replayed→chain revoked

Short access token, device-bound refresh token rotated on every use. A reused refresh token means theft: the whole chain is revoked.

"My devices" screen: paired devices listed, remote revoke, counted against the licence limit.

Losing the number

User Profile holds a verified email, for recovery and number changes.
Coach Number change via email: 24-48 h delay, alert to the old number and the email, one-click cancel. Extra proof: a past MoMo transaction reference. Single-use backup codes on activation.

Race 3 · Reverse Brainstorming

Play the Fraudster

How would you steal, cheat or abuse this app? Every attack on the left, its parry on the right. Tap the bar in the middle to block.

The owner made the better crook: 10 of the 13 attacks came from the user. The coach supplied most of the parries.

Attack COACH

One Expert subscription, H-10 screenshots resold in a WhatsApp group.

Parry

Invisible per-account watermark: a circulating capture names the leaking account (deterrent, and proof for suspension).

Attack USER

Copy the predictions onto paper and spread them on WhatsApp. "No technical defence possible."

Parry
  • Time: the value sits at H-10 to H-2. By the time it's copied, the odds have moved. The app recalculates live; the copy is frozen.
  • Personalisation: Kelly stake on your bankroll, Multi on your selection, the engine on your settings. A generic copy is worth less.
Attack USER

Film a subscribed friend's phone screen and share the video with a group of punters.

Parry
  • Block native capture/recording (FLAG_SECURE, iOS detection) on paid screens. Useless against a second phone, but it cuts the easiest share.
  • Semi-visible watermark (logo + short account ID) survives filming: the leak becomes an advert, and it traces the subscriber.
  • Accept a residual leak. The goal isn't zero leaks; it's that the subscription stays handier than a second-hand video.
Attack COACH

Pay a pass by MoMo, then dispute it with the operator and keep the access.

Parry

No confirmation ever comes from the app. Only the aggregator's signed webhook (signature checked + status API callback) grants a right. Idempotent per transaction reference. Daily licences-vs-payments reconciliation.

Attack USER

Use other subscribers' accounts and credentials.

Parry

New device = OTP to the holder's phone + a notification. Borrowing an account needs the holder's active help every time. Anomaly detection: two distant cities or IPs at once, frequent device rotation, then alert, then block.

Attack USER

Open the web account on several devices and browsers at the same time.

Parry

Every browser session counts as a device: "the web is unlimited" is not a loophole. User's call: over the limit, block the new device and offer Duo. Friendly sharing is sold to, not fought.

Attack USER

Hit the API directly and scrape the predictions without paying.

Parry

Every paid route checks token + entitlement (tier, dates) on the server. The server returns only what the tier allows; there is no hidden H-10 field on the client. A patched APK unlocks nothing. Rate limiting per account and per IP.

Attack USER

SQL injection.

Parry: a look at the code

SQLAlchemy ORM, so low risk. The only f-strings are migration ALTER TABLEs on internal names. But allow_origins=['*'] must be restricted, and SQLite (hippique.db) must give way to PostgreSQL.

Attack USER

Intercept the transactions.

Parry

HTTPS/TLS everywhere + certificate pinning in the mobile app. Payment trust only via the signed webhook.

Attack USER

Get at user accounts, transactions and predictions.

Parry

Separate schema or database for accounts and payments, minimal access, encrypted backups, store only what's needed (no card numbers, PIN never on the server). No keys in the APK (rule AD-3 extended to payment and SMS keys). Cameroon data-protection law added to the legal consultation.

"You can't stop information leaking. You make the copy worth less than the original."

Coach Turn the attack around: an affiliate programme for WhatsApp group admins (referral code, commission). The reseller becomes a distributor.
User Duo / Family offer kept. Over the device limit, block and propose Duo.

Photo finish · the coach's synthesis

What crossed the line

  1. 1FIRST

    One account, three jobs

    The account carries the licence, the PC-to-phone continuity and the anti-fraud defence in one go.

  2. 2SECOND

    The phone number is the spine, and the weak point

    Identity, Mobile Money, OTP and new-device checks all hang on it. That makes it a single point of failure, so email recovery is critical.

    identityMoMoOTPnew device↳ email recovery
  3. 3THIRD

    The anti-leak strategy is the product strategy

    Timing (H-10 for Expert) and personalisation are both the paywall and the defence against copying.

  4. 4FOURTH

    Inside or outside, one parry

    The server is the only authority: rights born from the webhook, tier filtering, audit. It stops the fraudster and the crooked agent alike.

  5. 5FIFTH

    The gap with what exists

    PRD and architecture are single-user with no account; the backend has no auth and runs on SQLite. Next: a correct-course, then PRD and architecture updates.
